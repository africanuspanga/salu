About SALU

An engineering partner for the systems everything else depends on.

We built SALU Solutions so that operators of power, telecom and industrial assets have one team that can design, supply, build and support the whole system, and answer for it.

Our story

When a feeder trips, a site goes dark or a line stops, the cost is counted in minutes. Too often the fix is slowed by too many contractors and no one owning the whole system.

SALU Solutions Limited brings power engineering, telecom works, automation, robotics and industrial supply into one company. Clients get engineers who understand how the pieces connect, technology from the world's leading manufacturers, and one point of accountability from survey to support.
